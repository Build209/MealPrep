Plans and prices

  • MealPlanatic has a free plan and three optional paid plans. Each plan includes a number of scans, recipe imports and Ask Forky questions every calendar month:
    • Free: 3 scans, 2 recipe imports and 5 Ask Forky questions a month.
    • Plus: 15 scans, 5 recipe imports and 15 Ask Forky questions a month. $4.99 a month or $53.88 a year.
    • Pro: 25 scans, 10 recipe imports and 30 Ask Forky questions a month. $8.99 a month or $93.84 a year.
    • Family: 50 scans, 25 recipe imports and 60 Ask Forky questions a month. $20 a month or $192 a year.
  • A scan is one photo of a pantry shelf, fridge or grocery receipt. An import is one recipe brought in from a link, pasted text, a photo, a screenshot or a video. Paid plans also include up to 200 store price-tag scans a month. Limits start again on the first day of each month (UTC) and unused ones do not carry over. One that ends in an error is given back; if one is lost another way, email us and we will restore it.

  • A paid plan renews automatically at the end of each month or year, at the price then in effect, until you cancel. We charge the payment method you gave at checkout through our payment processor, Stripe.
  • You can cancel at any time in Account → Manage subscription, or by emailing support@mealplanatic.app. Cancelling stops future charges. Your plan stays on until the end of the period you already paid for.
  • We do not give refunds for part of a period, except where the law requires it or where we charged you in error.
  • If we change the price, we will email you at least 7 days and no more than 30 days before the change, with how to cancel.
  • For yearly plans we email a reminder before each renewal.
  • If a payment fails, we may retry it. If it still fails, your paid plan ends and your account returns to the free plan. Your pantry, lists and recipes are kept.
  • Deleting your account cancels your paid plan straight away and stops future charges. Time left in a period you already paid for is not refunded.
