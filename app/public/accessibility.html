Accessibility

MealPlanatic · Last updated: October 15, 2026

Our goal

We want everyone to be able to plan meals with MealPlanatic. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA in the app and on this website.

What we do

  • Buttons and links are large enough to tap easily: at least 44 by 44 pixels.
  • We check our text and background colors for contrast.
  • We use a font designed to be easy to read (Atkinson Hyperlegible).
  • Buttons and links have labels that screen readers read out.
  • If you turn on reduced motion on your phone or computer, the app turns its animations off.

Known limits

  • The app has not yet had a full, independent accessibility audit. Some screens may still have problems.
  • Some pages are run by other companies and are outside our control, for example the Stripe checkout and billing pages.

Tell us about a problem

If something in MealPlanatic is hard to use, please email support@mealplanatic.app. Tell us what you were trying to do, the screen you were on, and the device and assistive technology you use, if any. We aim to reply within 5 business days.
