# Design second opinion

This wraps the audit list from Taste Skill's redesign skill (`reference/taste-redesign-audit.md`, MIT licence, copied unchanged). It was written for websites and landing pages, so parts of it do not fit a phone app. Use it this way:

1. Read `PRODUCT.md` and `DESIGN.md` at the repo root first. They win over anything in the checklist.
2. Read `reference/taste-redesign-audit.md` and use only its "Design Audit" section as a list of things to look for.
3. Report findings; change nothing unless asked. Rank them by effect on a person reaching the Plus screen or finishing a first scan.

Do not apply these parts of the checklist to MealPlanatic:

- Changing the typeface, the palette or the pill-and-card shapes. They are approved and binding.
- "Upgrade Techniques" (parallax, scroll effects, broken grids, noise, background images, placeholder photos). None belong in the app.
- Advice that only fits desktop websites (hover states as the main feedback, max-width containers, footers, sidebars).
- Replacing the icon set or avatars for the sake of difference.
- Anything that adds claims, reviews or numbers the product cannot back up.

It may be used more freely on a marketing or landing page, still inside the colours, typeface and voice in DESIGN.md.
